Trust

Evidence, not assurance.

The review is deliberately bounded. It is useful precisely because it shows where the analysis stops.

What it shows

  • Observed architecture changes
  • Supported local flow
  • Exact commit, path, blob and line
  • Coverage gaps and deferred detail

What it refuses to claim

  • Runtime behavior
  • Merge approval
  • Complete coverage of unsupported files
  • A substitute revision when evidence is missing
